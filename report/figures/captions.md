# Figure captions

Every figure here is a copy of a file produced by the script named in its row. The table behind each figure is the
CSV listed next to it. Win rates are from the NPC's side, with draws counted as ½. Error bars and bands are 95%
intervals unless the caption says otherwise.

| Figure | What it shows | Source file | Made by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `balance_winrate_heatmap.png` | Round-robin win-rate matrix over every archetype (train and held-out), the scripted NPC and a random player. 200 rounds per pair per seed, seeds 0–2, both sides, draws = ½. Used to check that no archetype dominates and that mirror matches sit near 50%. | `results/balance/winrate_heatmap.png` (table: `results/balance/winrate_matrix.csv`) | `scripts/balance_matrix.py` |
| `balance_draw_rate_heatmap.png` | Share of drawn rounds for each pair from the same round-robin. The passive archetypes (camper, repeater, mixer, random) mostly draw against each other. | `results/balance/draw_rate_heatmap.png` (table: `results/balance/draw_rate_matrix.csv`) | `scripts/balance_matrix.py` |
| `predictor_recovery_curve.png` | GRU predictor top-1 accuracy around a style switch, one panel per archetype switched into. The x-axis is steps since the switch. It shows how fast the predictor recovers when the player changes style. | `results/predictor/main/recovery_curve.png` (table: `recovery_curve.csv`) | `scripts/eval_predictor.py` |
| `learners_vs_scripted.png`, `learners_vs_ppo_none.png`, `learners_vs_ppo_predicted.png` | The online naive and reasoning learners against a frozen NPC (scripted, `ppo_none`, `ppo_predicted`). Per-round learner score, KL from the start policy, and policy entropy, one row per learner. Lines are means over matches, bands are 95% CIs, and the bold line is the frozen-control and chosen learning rate. The learners beat the scripted NPC, are level with `ppo_none`, and lose to `ppo_predicted`. | `results/learners/main/plots/<npc>.png` (tables: `outcome.csv`, `chosen.csv`, `drift.csv` in the same folder) | `scripts/check_learner_drift.py` |
| `adaptive_win_rate_by_round.png` | NPC win rate per round against the naive and reasoning learners (35 matches per point: 5 NPC seeds × 7) for `mbom`, `mbom_no_imagination` and `ppo_predicted`. All three decline as the learners adapt and the curves overlap within the noise. Development-set test (H1). | `results/mbom/adaptive_test/win_rate_by_round.png` (table: `win_rate_by_round.csv`) | `scripts/plot_adaptive_rounds.py` |
| `final_grid_claim_groups.png` | Final grid, pre-registered claim groups: NPC win rate against `exploiter_seed0` and `exploiter_seed2`, split into NPC seed 0 (the checkpoint the exploiters were trained against), seeds 1–4, and both exploiters pooled. One bar per method. MBOM is not separable from `ppo_predicted`, and `mbom_no_imagination` is slightly better than MBOM. | `results/m11/claim_groups.png` (table: `claim_groups.csv`; tests: `comparisons_table.csv`) | `scripts/make_plots.py` from `results/m10/final/` |
| `final_grid_early_late.png` | Win rate in rounds 1–5 (open circle) against the last 5 rounds (filled) per method, for the exploiter groups and the learners. Shows adaptation within a 30-round match. | `results/m11/early_late.png` (table: `early_late.csv`) | `scripts/make_plots.py` from `results/m10/final/` |
| `final_grid_alpha_traces.png` | MBOM mixer weights α on the three IOP levels (φ0, φ1, φ2) over the 30 rounds, mean over matches, with the α cap (≈ 0.79) marked. α settles on φ0 from about round 6 and stays at the cap, so the two best-response levels keep about 22% of the weight. | `results/m11/alpha_traces.png` (table: `alpha_traces.csv`) | `scripts/make_plots.py` from `results/m10/final/` |
